## Venue

The venue for the 2027 Benthic Ecology Meetings is the campus of [UMass Boston](https://www.umb.edu/). The welcome social well be held Monday evening at the [Integrated Sciences Complex](https://www.umb.edu/campus-planning/projects/integrated-sciences-complex/) and the meeting talks, lunch, and poster session will be held in the [Campus Center](https://www.umb.edu/campus-center/), right on the edge of Boston Harbor.

## Hotel

We will have two official hotels and venue for BEM 2027 is the [Marriott Virginia Beach Oceanfront Resort](https://www.cavalierresortvb.com/marriott-virginia-beach-oceanfront-resort).

### Second Hotel TBD!

We have one more hotel coming. It will be on the MBTA Red Line, and thus attendees will be able to take a quick trip to the JFK/UMass MBTA stop, where shuttles will take you straight to the front doors of the Campus Center or around the corner to the ISC.


## Transportation

Boston is easily accessible by flying in to Boston Logan airport or by taking the [Amtrak](https://www.amtrak.com/) Northeast Regional or Acela to South Station if coming from South of Boston or on the Downeaster to North Station if coming from North. [Commuter rail](https://www.mbta.com/schedules/commuter-rail) options are available as well for those coming from Western Massachusetts, Rhode Island, and southern New Hampshire. The southern commuter rail lines even stop at the JFK/UMass MBTA stop where you can board a shuttle to campus.

The Doubletree provides free shuttle service to and from Boston Logan airport from 6am to 10pm. Upon arrival, text 617-433-9800 for pickup.
