## Sponsor Benthics!

The Benthic Ecology Meeting is an incredible venue for science. It's a place where scientists come to share the cutting edge of their research, form new transformative collaborations, and advance the field. Moreover, it is a very student centered meeting. Benthics is an deeply important venue for the professional growth and advancement of its student members. Many students - graduate and undergraduate - give the first talks of their careers at Benthics, entering the larger conversation of science. Others might give a talk that will break scientific paradigms and lead to real change in research or policy. All look to Benthics as a time for meaningful professional networking and helping to figure out the next step of their careers.

We ask that you help support our meeting and all of the good it does for science and society. If you are interested in sponsoring the meeting, please contact [bem2027@benthics.org](mailto:bem2027@benthics.org).
