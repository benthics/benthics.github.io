## Your Hosts

The 52nd BEM is hosted by Clemson University and College of Charleston, both located in the state of South Carolina. They are home to over 30,000 students.

---

**Clemson University was founded in 1889 and it is:**

<img src="/images/Clemson.jpg" alt="Clemson University" width="450" style="float:right; margin: 0 0 1rem 2rem;">

- Ranked as a R1: Doctoral University – Highest Research Activity by the Carnegie Classification of Institutions of Higher Education
- Ranked as the top national public university in South Carolina in 2023 by the U.S. News & World Report annual rankings
- Ranked No. 31 among national public institutions out of 1500 colleges, according to the U.S. News & World Report annual rankings

<div style="clear:both;"></div>

---

**College of Charleston was founded in 1770 and it is:**

<img src="/images/CofC campus.jpg" alt="College of Charleston" width="250" style="float:left; margin: 0 2rem 1rem 0;">

- A nationally recognized, public liberal arts and sciences university
- Ranked 9th among regional universities in the South by the U.S. News & World Report annual rankings
- Ranked 7th in Best Undergraduate Teaching in the South by the U.S. News & World Report annual rankings

<div style="clear:both;"></div>

## Conference Organizers

Following tradition, the President of the Benthic Ecology Society also serves as the organizer for the Benthic Ecology Meeting for that year. The 52nd BEM is organized by Michael Childress and J. Antonio Baeza (Clemson) together with Erik Sotka and Craig Plante (College of Charleston).
